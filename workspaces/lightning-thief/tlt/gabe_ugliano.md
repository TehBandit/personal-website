Gabe Ugliano, nicknamed 'Smelly Gabe' by Percy, is Percy's stepfather and is characterized by his unpleasant demeanor and habits. He is described as reeking of moldy garlic pizza and stale beer, and he treats Sally and Percy poorly. Gabe is a gambler who often demands money from Percy to fund his poker games, threatening him to keep it a 'guy secret.' He is dismissive and rude, showing little interest in Percy's life or well-being. Gabe manages the Electronics Mega-Mart in Queens but spends most of his time at home, indulging in cigars and beer. Despite his negative traits, Sally tries to appease him to maintain peace, especially when planning trips like the one to Montauk. His presence looms over their lives, creating a difficult environment for both Sally and Percy.
