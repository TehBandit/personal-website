Percy Jackson is a twelve-year-old boy who discovers he is a half-blood.

Percy Jackson is a twelve-year-old boy navigating the tumultuous waters of adolescence at Yancy Academy, a school for troubled kids in upstate New York. Recently, he has come to terms with the fact that he is a half-blood, a revelation that fills him with both dread and confusion. His life takes a bizarre turn during a field trip to the Metropolitan Museum of Art, where he confronts his math teacher, Mrs. Dodds, who unexpectedly transforms into a monstrous creature. Armed with a pen that transforms into a sword, a gift from his favorite Latin teacher, Mr. Brunner, Percy manages to defeat her. However, the strangeness escalates when he realizes that no one else remembers Mrs. Dodds, leaving him questioning his own sanity. As he grapples with hallucinations and strange weather phenomena, including a thunderstorm that shatters his dorm windows, Percy finds himself expelled from Yancy Academy. Despite his troubles, he cherishes his friendship with Grover Underwood, who stands by him through thick and thin, even as Percy faces the bullying of Nancy Bobofit. The tension mounts when Percy overhears Grover and Mr. Brunner discussing his safety and a mysterious deadline related to the summer solstice, hinting at dangers that lie ahead. On a bus ride with Grover, they encounter three old ladies knitting enormous socks, an unsettling sight that Grover seems to fear, suggesting that their presence is tied to the ominous events unfolding around Percy. Haunted by memories of strange occurrences from his past, including a man with one eye and a snake incident in preschool, Percy feels a mix of resentment and curiosity about his absent father, who he knows little about except that he was 'lost at sea.' His relationship with his mother, Sally Jackson, is deeply affectionate, and he is protective of her, especially against the mistreatment from his stepfather, Gabe Ugliano, whom he nicknames 'Smelly Gabe.' As Percy navigates these challenges, he is increasingly aware of the supernatural elements surrounding him, culminating in a storm at Montauk that marks a significant turning point in his life. Percy's journey is marked by a series of revelations about his identity and the mythological world he is part of. He learns that his life is in danger due to his true nature, which is connected to ancient myths and powerful beings. The encounter with the Minotaur is a turning point, as it forces Percy to confront the reality of his heritage and the threats that come with it. Despite the loss of his mother, Percy demonstrates bravery and resourcefulness, qualities that will be crucial as he navigates his new reality.
