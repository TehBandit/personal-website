Mr. Brunner is Percy's Latin teacher who leads the museum trip.

Mr. Brunner, Percy's Latin teacher at Yancy Academy, is a figure of authority and wisdom who captivates his students with his engaging teaching style and passion for Roman history. He leads a pivotal field trip to the Metropolitan Museum of Art, where he encourages Percy to explore the relevance of Greek mythology in the modern world. During a critical moment, Mr. Brunner aids Percy by throwing him a pen that magically transforms into a sword, enabling him to defend himself against the monstrous Mrs. Dodds. Despite his apparent knowledge of the supernatural events surrounding Percy, Mr. Brunner later acts as if Mrs. Dodds never existed, leaving Percy in a state of confusion. He is involved in a secretive discussion with Grover about Percy's safety, indicating that he is aware of the dangers that lie ahead. Mr. Brunner believes in Percy's potential and emphasizes the importance of Latin as a life-and-death subject for him. However, he ultimately advises Grover to let Percy remain oblivious to the larger forces at play, suggesting that there are significant challenges ahead that Percy is not yet ready to confront.
