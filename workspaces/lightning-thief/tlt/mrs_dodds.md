Mrs. Dodds is a math teacher who transforms into a monstrous creature.

Mrs. Dodds is a math teacher at Yancy Academy, known for her mean demeanor and her particular dislike for Percy Jackson. She often gives him detention and seems to take pleasure in making his life difficult. During the field trip to the museum, Mrs. Dodds reveals her true nature when she transforms into a terrifying creature with glowing eyes, talons, and leathery wings. She confronts Percy, accusing him of causing trouble and attempting to attack him. In a moment of desperation, Percy uses the sword given to him by Mr. Brunner to vaporize her. Following this incident, Percy is left in a state of confusion when no one else remembers Mrs. Dodds, prompting him to question the reality of what he has experienced.
