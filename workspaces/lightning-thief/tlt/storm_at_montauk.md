A violent storm hits Montauk, marking a turning point in Percy's life.

The storm at Montauk is a pivotal event that coincides with Percy's vivid dream of a horse and an eagle fighting. This storm is unusual for the time of year and is accompanied by a sense of foreboding. It is during this storm that Grover arrives at the cabin, revealing his true nature as a satyr and warning of impending danger. The storm serves as a catalyst for the unfolding of Percy's journey into the world of Greek mythology, as it prompts his mother to take immediate action to protect him.
