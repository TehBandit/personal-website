Sally Jackson dreams of becoming a novelist.

Sally Jackson harbors dreams of becoming a novelist, a passion she had to set aside due to life's hardships. Despite losing her parents at a young age and having to care for an uncaring uncle, Sally worked hard to save money for college. However, her uncle's illness forced her to abandon her education. Her dreams are a recurring theme in her life, symbolizing her resilience and hope for a better future. She shares these dreams with Percy during their time at Montauk, revealing her aspirations and the sacrifices she has made for her family.
