**<u>Managing Change</u>**

- *Uncertainty*
  - **Lewin’s Change Model**
    - **Unfreezing** - Getting people to recognize that change is necessary
      - Deliver the need for change with a **message of hope**
    - **Change Intervention** - Providing the tools and support needed for change
    - **Refreezing** - Sustaining the change
      - Making it stick
      - Monitor change
        - Correct people that are not adhering to change
  - **Managing Resistance to Change**
    - Start early
      - Get people to believe the change is necessary
    - Gain Allies
      - Can choose to “pour water, or gasoline” onto small fires (resistance)
      - Don’t fuel rumors and dissent
  - Change is related to **Innovation**
    - An essential element of **Entrepreneurship**
