**<u>Decision Making</u>**

- *Decision Making*
  - **Decisions** - A choice made from among **available** alternative options
    - **Decision Making** - the Process of identifying and choosing from alternatives
      - **System 1** - Intuitive and Unconscious
        - Instinctual
      - **System 2** - Analytical and Conscious
        - Deliberate
    - **Rational Model of Decision Making** - Assumes managers will make logical decisions that are optimal for the firm
      - The *Classical* Model
      - **Prescriptive** - Describes how managers should make decisions, not how they do
      - **1 - Identify the Problem**
        - Difficulty that inhibits achievement of goals
        - Also finds **opportunities**
          - Situations that present possibility to exceed goals
      - **2 - Think of Alternatives**
      - **3 - Evaluate Alternatives**
        - Must be **ethical**, **feasible**, and **effective**
      - **4 - Implement and Evaluate Solution**
    - **Nonrational Model of Decision Making** - Assumes decision making is uncertain and risky
      - Describes how managers actually make decisions
      - **Bounded Rationality** - Ability to be rational is limited by many constraints
      - **Satisficing** - “good enough”
      - **Intuition** - Choice made without conscious thought or logical reasoning
    - **Evidence Based Decision Making**
      - **Analytics** - Business data analysis
        - **Predictive Modeling** - Data mining to predict future behavior
        - **Big Data** - Data too large to be analyzed by conventional methods
    - **Decision Making Styles**
      - Value Orientation vs Tolerance for Ambiguity
        - **Directive** - Low tolerance for ambiguity
        - **Analytical** - High tolerance for ambiguity
        - **Conceptual** - High tolerance for ambiguity + Value of people
        - **Behavioral Style** - Value of people
    - **Decision Making and AI**
      - **Heuristics** - Strategies people use to simplify the process of making decisions
        - Biases
          - Availability - People recall more recent things better
          - Representative - Generalizing from a small sample or single event
          - Confirmation bias - Discounts data that does not support your views
          - Sunk-Cost - Deciding a project has been too costly to abandon
          - Anchoring - Making decisions
          - Overconfidence Bias
          - Hindsight Bias - People view things as more predictable than they really are
          - Framing Bias - 85% lean vs 15% fat
          - Escalation of Commitment - Refusing to admit failure
          - **Discounting the Future** - Choosing a choice with immediate benefit, knowing that it will be negative in the future
            - *Ex. Smoking*
      - Types of AI
        - Assisted intelligence - Automates basic tasks
        - Augmented Intelligence - Automates other tasks robotically
        - Autonomous intelligence - Machine learning
