**<u>Planning and Strategy</u>**

- *Planning, Strategy, and Strategic Management*
  - **Planning** - Setting goals AND deciding a strategy how to achieve them
    - Coping with uncertainty by formulating future courses of action
    - **Plan** - Outlines how goals are going to be met
      - **Business Plan** - Outlines a firm’s goals, a strategy for achieving them, and ways to measure success
        - Describes the firm's **business model**
          - Describes the niche the firm fills, its functions, and expected revenues and expenses
      - **Developing Plans**
        - SMART Goals
          - Objectives are jointly established
        - Commitment
        - Action Plans
        - Monitor
          - Progress periodically reviewed, rewards linked
        - Flexible
  - **Strategy** - Sets the long term goals and direction for an organization
  - **Strategic Management** - Involves managers from all parts of the organization in the formulation and implementation of strategic goals
    - Involved both top and middle managers
    - *Why?*
      - Provide direction and momentum
        - Prevents surprises and gives a long term goal to work towards
      - Encourage new ideas
      - Develop a sustainable competitive advantage
- *Fundamentals of Planning*
  - **Mission** - A firm’s purpose
    - **Mission statement** - Expresses the firm’s mission
      - Major products/services, customer base, technology, basic beliefs and attitudes
  - **Vision** - Where a firm wants to go
    - **Vision Statement** - Expresses the firm’s vision
  - The mission and the vision comprise a firm’s **values**
  - **Strategic Planning**
    - Done by top management using mission and vision statements
    - Long term
    - **Tactical Planning** 
      - Done by Middle Management from the strategy passed down by top management
      - 6-24 Months
      - **Operational Planning**
        - Done by front-line managers
        - 1-52 weeks
- *Goals and Plans*
  - **Objective** - a specific commitment to achieve a measurable result within a stated time frame
    - Also called **goals**
    - **SMART Goals** - Specific, measurable, attainable, results-oriented, and has target dates
    - **Cascading Goals** - Top level strategic goals cascade downward into more specific achievable goals as they trickle down
    - **Strategic Goals**
      - Long term
      - Tackle issues covered in strategic plan
    - **Tactical / Operational Goals**
      - Short term
        - Generally span one year
      - Connected to strategic goals through a **means-end chain**
        - Shows how goals are linked to a company’s strategy
  - **Operating Plan** - Breaks long term output into short term goals
  - **Action Plan** - Defined the course of action needed to achieve a stated goal
  - **Plans**
    - **Standing Plans**
      - Repeated or occur frequently over time
      - Include **policies**, **procedures,** and **rules**
      - *Ex. A standard return policy*
    - **Single-Use Plans**
      - Single-use
      - Include **programs** and **projects**
- *Motivating Employees*
  - **Management by Objectives** *(MBO)*
    - Managers and employees jointly set objectives
    - Managers develop action plans
    - Managers and employees periodically review performance
    - Managers makes a performance appraisal and rewards employee accordingly
- *Planning / Control Cycle*
  - **Planning**
    - Make the Plan
    - Carry out the Plan
  - **Controlling**
    - Compare Results
    - Take corrective action
