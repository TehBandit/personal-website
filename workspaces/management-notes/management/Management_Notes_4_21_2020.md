**<u>Leadership</u>**

- *Effective Leaders*
  - Managers - People with a title
  - **Leaders** - People would follow even without the title
    - **Attitude**
      - Attitude reflects leadership
      - **Positive** outlook
      - Importance of **humility** as a leadership quality
      - Strong **work ethic**
    - **Behavior** - Make other **people** feel important	
      - People focused
      - Take focus
      - Deliver results
        - Do impressive things
        - Hold people accountable
        - Make difficult decisions
        - Overcome obstacles
    - Character
      - **Integrity** - Doing the right thing when nobody's looking
      - Test is to give someone power
        - Leader does not **command** authority
          - **Shows** them what to do and **helps** them get there
        - Leadership has a **cost**
          - Can’t take easy way out, must actively choose to be a good person
          - Cost is **self-interest**
            - Must put others above yourself
