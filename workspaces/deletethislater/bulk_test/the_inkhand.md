THE INKHAND

The Inkhand is a name that appears in accounts of the Tide Compact as its third founding member, a broker or intermediary whose actual identity has never been established. What is known — or at least consistently reported — is as follows.

The Inkhand communicates exclusively through written notes, using a distinctive reddish ink that some sources describe as rust-colored and others as dried-blood dark. The notes are always unsigned except for a small pressed mark: a hand, palm-forward, rendered in the same ink. Recipients describe the handwriting as precise and unhurried, the kind produced by someone with formal scribal training.

The Inkhand is believed to have access to documents that would not normally be available outside of institutional archives — specifically, Admiralty survey data and, in at least one instance, an internal record from the Deepwater Guild that predated its official circulation by several days. This has led to speculation that the Inkhand is an archivist or scribe employed by one of these institutions, using their position to supply the Compact with information it can sell or use for leverage.

Rella Corse, when asked about the Inkhand directly, has said only that the arrangement is functional and that she does not ask questions she doesn't need answers to. Dovan Strake, when asked the same question over drinks at the Wrecked Compass, became briefly less jovial than usual and changed the subject.

The name Maren Ashveil has been mentioned in connection with the Inkhand by one source of uncertain reliability. This connection has not been corroborated. Maren Ashveil, if asked, would presumably deny it. Whether anyone has thought to ask is unclear.

The Inkhand has not been seen — or at least, has not been identified as the Inkhand — by anyone who has subsequently confirmed the identification. It is possible the Inkhand is more than one person. It is possible the Inkhand is a fiction the Compact maintains to imply resources it does not have. It is also possible the Inkhand is someone that several people in the harbor district have met without knowing who they were talking to.
