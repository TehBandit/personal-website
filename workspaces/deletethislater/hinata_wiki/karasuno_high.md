KARASUNO HIGH — location notes

Karasuno High is the school where Hinata attends as a first-year student.
It is known for its Boys' Volleyball Team, where Hinata plays as a middle blocker.
The school is associated with the Small Giant, who inspired Hinata.
Karasuno is known as the 'Flightless Crows' but aims to change that perception.
The team participates in tournaments and faces strong opponents like Date Tech and Aoba Johsai.
Karasuno's matches are broadcasted on TV, gaining them recognition.
Karasuno wins the first set against Ohgiminami easily.
Karasuno wins the match against Kakugawa with a score of 2:0.
Karasuno advances to the Representative Playoffs after winning the Miyagi Prefecture's first Spring High Preliminary.
