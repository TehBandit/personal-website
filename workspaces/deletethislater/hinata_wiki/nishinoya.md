NISHINOYA — character notes

Nishinoya is introduced to the first-years by Daichi.
He is shorter than Hinata, which initially surprises Hinata.
Nishinoya is under house arrest and banned from club activities for a month.
He is passionate about volleyball and insults Asahi for not returning to the team.
Nishinoya agrees to teach Hinata how to receive after being called 'senpai'.
He demonstrates his 'Rolling Thunder' move, impressing Hinata.
Nishinoya tries to cheer Hinata up about being a decoy.
He participates in the practice match against the Neighborhood Association Team.
Nishinoya is mistaken for a child by Hinata and Tanaka due to his flattened hair.
