DAICHI SAWAMURA — character notes

Daichi Sawamura is the captain of Karasuno's volleyball team.
He is responsible for maintaining discipline among the team members.
Daichi throws Hinata and Kageyama out of the gym for causing trouble.
Daichi introduces the first-years to Nishinoya.
He is part of the team that faces the Neighborhood Association Team in a practice match.
Daichi is involved in the practice match against Nekoma.
Daichi encourages the team for its next match against Kakugawa.
He advises Hinata and Tanaka to use a soft block against Hyakuzawa.
