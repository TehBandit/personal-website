ASAHI — character notes

Asahi is hesitant to return to volleyball due to a past defeat.
He is insulted by Nishinoya for not returning to the team.
Asahi is surprised by Hinata's determination to become an ace.
He is pulled into a practice match by Ukai and faces off against Hinata and Kageyama.
Asahi's powerful spikes impress Hinata but also make him jealous.
He reassures Hinata of his importance as a decoy after the match.
Asahi is approached by Hinata and Kageyama multiple times to rejoin the team.
Asahi is challenged by Aone but is supported by Hinata.
He uses scary thoughts to calm himself before matches.
Asahi is motivated to get revenge against Date Tech for a past defeat.
He benefits from Hinata's decoy skills to score points.
Asahi holds Hinata up to the team after their victory.
