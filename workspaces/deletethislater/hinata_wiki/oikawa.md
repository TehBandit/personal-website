OIKAWA — character notes

Oikawa enters the gym during the Aoba Johsai match, impressing Hinata.
Oikawa is introduced by Kageyama as his former senpai.
Oikawa scores several points off Tsukishima with his serves.
Oikawa compliments Hinata on his skills after the match.
Oikawa greets Hinata and Kageyama with hostility.
He figures out Kageyama and Hinata's signals, rendering their quick strike ineffective.
Oikawa expresses a desire to destroy Karasuno, angering the team.
