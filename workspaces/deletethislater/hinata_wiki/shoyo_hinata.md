**Shōyō Hinata** is the main protagonist of the Haikyū!! series by Haruichi Furudate. Hinata was a first-year student at Karasuno High and a middle blocker for the Boys' Volleyball Team. In 2018, he joined MSBY Black Jackals, a Division 1 team in the V-League. He became a member of Japan Men's National Volleyball Team during the 2021 Olympics. As of 2022, Hinata is a member of Asas São Paulo in the Brazil Super League. Hinata is the older brother of Natsu Hinata. Upon entering high school, Hinata was 162.8 cm tall and grew to 164.2 cm. He is the second shortest person on the Karasuno volleyball team. Hinata has unruly orange hair and brown eyes. He typically wears his Karasuno school uniform and a black volleyball jersey with number 10. Post-timeskip, Hinata grew his hair out and trained in Brazil, reaching a height of 172 cm. Hinata has a cheerful and energetic personality, easily excited and loud. He is simpleminded but observant, empathetic, and a natural schmoozer. Hinata has a serious attitude towards volleyball and plays with great tenacity. He gets scared easily but recovers quickly to challenge opponents. Initially, Hinata was nervous before matches but improved over time. Hinata struggles academically but improves with motivation and guidance.
