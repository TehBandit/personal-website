KENMA KOZUME — character notes

Kenma meets Hinata while Hinata is lost during a jog.
He is initially reluctant to talk but engages in a conversation about volleyball with Hinata.
Kenma is a setter and shares a similar situation with Hinata regarding height.
He is part of the Nekoma team that faces Karasuno in a practice match.
Kenma surprises Hinata by being on the Nekoma team during the practice match.
