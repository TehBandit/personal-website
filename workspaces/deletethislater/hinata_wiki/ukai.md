UKAI — character notes

Ukai is admired by Kageyama, who joined Karasuno because of him.
He sets up a practice match against the Neighborhood Association Team.
Ukai pulls Asahi into the practice match to face off against Hinata and Kageyama.
He advises Kageyama and Hinata during the practice match against Nekoma.
Ukai encourages the team to eat well and recover after matches.
He makes strategic decisions, such as switching players during games.
Ukai holds a meeting in the gym and informs Karasuno about their loss to Aoba Johsai and Shiratorizawa's victory.
He is present during discussions about Karasuno's plans and training.
Ukai initially agrees with Kageyama's viewpoint on Hinata's quick strike.
He changes his mind after seeing Hinata's determination and supports his idea.
Ukai arranges for a practice match between Hinata and Kageyama's teams.
He takes Hinata to the original Coach Ikkei Ukai for further training.
Ukai is affected by Ikkei's words about the spiker's role in a quick strike.
He continues to support Hinata and Kageyama's development.
