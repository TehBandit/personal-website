- *Project Management*
  - **Project Management** - The application of knowledge, skills, tools, and techniques to project activities to meet project requirement
  - **Project** - A temporary endeavor undertaken to create a unique product, service, or result
    - **Temporary**
    - End when output is produced
    - Fixed Budget
    - **Attributes of Projects**
      - Projects have a **unique purpose**
        - *Ex. build THIS specific house with THESE requirements*
      - **Temporary**, definite beginning and end
      - Drive change, or enable value creation
      - Developed **iteratively**
      - Require **resources**
      - Have a **primary customer or sponsor**
        - Someone responsible for direction and funding of the project
      - Involve **uncertainty**
    - **Project Constraints**
      - **Triple Constraint**
        - **Scope** - What needs to be done and how should it be done?
        - **Schedule** - How long should it take to be completed?
        - **Cost** - What is the budget? What resources should be needed?
      - **Quality** 
        - Sometimes included in the **Quadruple Constraint**
      - **Risk**
      - **Resources**
    - **Project Stakeholders** - Any person involved in or affected by the project
      - *Ex. Couple wanting to build a home*
        - **Project Sponsor** - Responsible for direction and funding of the project
          - *Couple*
        - **Project Manager** - Lead the operations of the project
          - *Contractor*
        - **Project Team** - Those involved in Project completion
          - *Employees*
        - **Support Staff** - Support project team
          - *Business office workers*
        - **Suppliers**
        - Additional Stakeholders
        - Opponents
    - **Defining Project Success**
      - Did the project meet the triple constraint?
      - Was the sponsor satisfied?
        - Can also be measured by customer rating system
      - Did the project produce the desired results?
        - May not be immediately known if desired results are long term
    - Not everything is a project, some things are **Operations**
      - Ongoing/permanent activities
      - Help sustain business, make profit
      - Day-to-day things
  - **Programs and Portfolios**
    - **Program** - a group of related projects managed in a coordinated manner to obtain benefits that would not be apparent if managed individually
      - *Ex. A construction company building small homes and large office buildings at the same time*
      - Not necessarily a **megaproject,** (one large >$1bn project)
    - **Portfolio Management -** The way in which organization group and manage projects and programs
      - Concerned not just with grouping projects, but with choosing the *right* projects to undertake
      - Generally done by higher level employees
- *Doing Project Management*
  - **Process Groups**
    - Initiating
    - Planning
    - Executing
    - Monitoring/Controlling
    - Closing
  - **Knowledge Areas**
    - Integration Management
      - How the project fits together
        - Project management software, change requests, lesson-learned reports
    - Scope Management
      - Score statements, work breakdown structures, requirements analyses
    - Schedule
      - PERT, GANTT charts
    - Cost
    - Quality
    - Resources
    - Communication
      - Kickoff meetings, status and progress reports
    - Risk
    - Procurement
    - Stakeholders
  - **Project Managers**
    - Should be proficient in all 10 knowledge areas
    - Proficiency in **application area**
    - Knowledge of **Project Environment**
      - Politics, culture
    - General business knowledge
    - Human relations
      - Soft skills increasingly important today
    - **Talent Triangle**
      - Technical Skills
      - Soft Skills
        - **Leader** - Focuses on long term goals and inspires people
          - **Leadership Styles**
            - **Laissez-Faire** - Hands off
            - **Transactional** - Step in to offer reward or reinforcement
            - **Servant** - Focus on building community
            - **Transformational** - Inspire others and empower them to get things done
            - **Charismatic** - Lead by enthusiasm
            - **Interactional** - Transactional, transformational, AND charismatic
        - **Manager** - Day to day details of meeting goals
      - Strategic and business skills
    - **Certifications**
      - **Prince2** - UK/AU
      - **PMP** - USA
        - 4500 hours of experience
      - **CAPM** 
        - 1500 hours of experience or 23 hours of training
