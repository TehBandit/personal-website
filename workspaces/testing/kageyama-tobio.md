# Kageyama Tobio

Kageyama Tobio is the son of Akaashi Keiji. He is described as having a serious demeanor and a scowl that seems too intense for his age. He is passionate about volleyball and aspires to be the best setter. Kageyama is competitive, often telling Shouyou that he 'sucks,' but he also shows admiration for Bokuto's volleyball skills.

Kageyama has a close and complex relationship with Hinata, characterized by playful banter and mutual support. He often scolds Hinata but also shows care, such as when he wraps Hinata in jackets during cold weather. Their relationship evolves to include romantic elements, as seen when Kageyama kisses Hinata during practice.
