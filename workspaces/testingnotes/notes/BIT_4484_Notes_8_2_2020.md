**<u>Chapter 6 - Project Planning pt3</u>**

- ***Remaining Knowledge Areas***
  - **Project Quality Management**
    - **Quality** - Degree to which set of inherent characteristics fulfills requirements
      - **Conformance to requirements** - Processes and products meet written specifications
      - **Fitness for use** - Product or process can be used as intended
      - Customer decides if quality is satisfactory
      - Many fail cause they consider only the written requirements, ignore other stakeholder needs and expectations.
      - **Quality Management Plan** - Identifying quality standards and how to meet them
        - **Metric** - Standard of metric
          - Failure rate, availability, customer satisfaction ratings
          - **Schedule** - Completing the project on time
          - Customer Satisfaction
          - **Cost Reduction** - Recouping project costs
          - **Project Dashboard** - Keeps track of all metrics on one graphic
  - **Project Resource Management**
    - Project Organizational Chart - Keeps track of who does what
    - Responsibility Assignment Matrix - Maps the tasks of the WBS to people responsible for performing that work
      - RACI Chart
        - **Responsible** - Does the Task
          - Only one per task
        - **Accountable** - Signs off on the task
        - **Consulted** - Has information needed to complete task
        - **Informed** - need to be notified of task results
    - **Staffing Management Plan** - Described how people are added and removed from a project
    - **Team Charter** - Promotes teamwork and clarifies how team will communicate
      - Can be completed within a 1-2hr meeting
      - What do we do if there is a problem?
  - **Project Communications Management**
    - **Communications Management Plan** - Document that guides communication
  - **Project Stakeholder Management**
  - **Project Risk Management**
    - Uncertainty that can have a positive or negative effect on meeting objectives
    - Cannot manage risks until you **identify them**
      - **Probability Impact Matrix** - Classifies risks by likelihood and how bad they will be
        - Top right is the worst
        - Low left, no matter
      - **Risk Avoidance** - Eliminate causes of risk
      - **Risk Acceptance** - Accepting the consequences, if the risk occurs
      - **Risk Transference** - Transfer consequences to a third party
        - Insurance
      - **Risk Mitigation** - Reduce Likelihood
      - **Positive Risks**
        - **Risk Exploitation** - Trying to make sure a risk happens
        - **Risk Sharing** - Allocation ownership of risk to another party
        - **Risk enhancement** - Increase size of opportunity
        - **Risk Acceptance** - Dont care to seize opportunity, if it happens it happens
    - **Contingency Plan** - Outlined actions if a specific risk event occurs
    - **Fallback Plan** - For risks that have a **high impact** on meeting objectives. Last resort
    - Contingency Reserves - Money to alleviate effects of risks
      - Allowances
  - **Project Procurement Management**
    - **Make-or-Buy Analysis** - Produce internally or outsource?
    - **Contract**
      - **Termination Clause** - How to get out of contract, what will happen if you do
      - **Incentive Clause** - Gives bonuses for behaving in good ways
      - **Liquidated Damages Clause** - What happens if seller does not do what supposed to do
      - **Fixed Price Contract** - Seller provides product at a fixed price, does not rise/fall with market price
      - **Competitive Contract** - Many given small task, best performers get big contract
      - **Cost Reimbursable** - Pay some directly, and some indirect costs
        - Electricity, rent, etc
      - **Time and Material** - Pay a fixed price for materials, and an hourly wage for labor
      - **Unit Price** - Predetermined price per unit of service
    - **Procurement Documents**
      - **Request for Proposal (RFP**) - Seller offers a proposal on how they will  meet your goals
        - Open ended
      - **Request for Quote (RFQ)** - Sellers offers a price for what they will provide
        - Predefined
      - **Statement of Work (SOW)** - What you need provided
    - **Supplier Evaluation Matrix** - Weights the suppliers based on different weighted categories
