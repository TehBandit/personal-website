As Percy, his mother, and Grover drive through the night, they are caught in a violent storm on Long Island. The storm is characterized by heavy rain, strong winds, and frequent lightning, which adds to the tension and urgency of their escape. The storm is so intense that it causes their car to crash into a ditch, leaving them vulnerable to the pursuing Minotaur. This storm serves as a backdrop to the unfolding drama and heightens the sense of danger and chaos as they attempt to reach the safety of the camp.
