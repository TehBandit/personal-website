Percy Jackson is a twelve-year-old boy who has recently come to terms with his identity as a half-blood, a realization that fills him with a sense of danger and uncertainty. He attends Yancy Academy, a school for troubled kids in upstate New York, where his life takes a dramatic turn during a field trip to the Metropolitan Museum of Art. There, he encounters Mrs. Dodds, a teacher who unexpectedly transforms into a monstrous creature, forcing Percy to defend himself with a pen that magically becomes a sword. This shocking event leaves him bewildered, especially when his best friend Grover and his trusted teacher Mr. Brunner deny that Mrs. Dodds ever existed, leading Percy to question his own sanity. As he grapples with visions of Mrs. Dodds and a growing sense of foreboding, he overhears Grover and Mr. Brunner discussing his safety and a mysterious deadline related to the summer solstice. The tension escalates when he and Grover encounter three old ladies knitting enormous socks, further deepening Percy's confusion about his identity and the world around him. Despite his struggles with dyslexia and ADHD, Percy is deeply protective of his mother, Sally Jackson, whom he describes as the best person in the world, and he harbors resentment towards his stepfather, Gabe Ugliano, who adds to the tension in his life. During a stormy night at their cabin in Montauk, Percy experiences a vivid dream that foreshadows the tumultuous events to come, and Grover's unexpected arrival reveals the urgency of the situation, prompting Percy and his mother to flee. As they race through the night, they are pursued by a monstrous figure, the Minotaur, which captures his mother, leaving Percy devastated but determined to fight back and protect his friend Grover.
