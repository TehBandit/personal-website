Sally Jackson is Percy's mother, described as the best person in the world.

Sally Jackson is Percy's loving and supportive mother, who has faced numerous hardships in her life, including the loss of her parents in a plane crash and the responsibility of caring for her sick uncle. Despite these challenges, she has raised Percy on her own, working odd jobs and taking night classes to earn her high school diploma. Sally eventually married Gabe Ugliano, a man who turned out to be a source of stress and unhappiness in her life. She maintains a rebellious streak, as seen in her insistence on eating blue food to defy Gabe's claim that it doesn't exist. Sally is protective of Percy and is aware of the dangers he faces, though she is reluctant to send him to a special camp his father had suggested. During their vacation in Montauk, she seems to rejuvenate, shedding years of worry and work, and her determination to keep Percy safe becomes paramount during the violent storm that threatens them. As they drive through the night, Sally reveals that she cannot cross the property line to the safety of the camp, indicating a deeper knowledge of the magical world. In a heartbreaking moment during the encounter with the Minotaur, she is captured and vanishes in a flash of golden light, leaving Percy devastated and propelling him into action as he embarks on his journey into the world of Greek mythology.
