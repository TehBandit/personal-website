Mr. Brunner is Percy's Latin teacher at Yancy Academy.

Mr. Brunner, Percy's Latin teacher at Yancy Academy, is a figure shrouded in mystery and wisdom. Known for his engaging teaching style and his impressive collection of Roman armor and weapons, he plays a crucial role during the field trip to the Metropolitan Museum of Art. When chaos erupts with Mrs. Dodds, Mr. Brunner throws Percy a pen that transforms into a sword, enabling him to defend himself against the monstrous teacher. However, his subsequent denial of Mrs. Dodds' existence only deepens Percy's confusion about the reality he is experiencing. Mr. Brunner's conversations with Grover about the summer solstice and the need to protect Percy suggest that he is aware of the supernatural elements surrounding his student. His belief in Percy and acknowledgment of his unique destiny hint at a larger narrative at play, indicating that Yancy Academy may not be the right place for him as he embarks on a journey of self-discovery.
