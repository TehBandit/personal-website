Gabe Ugliano, often referred to by Percy as 'Smelly Gabe,' is Percy's stepfather and a constant source of tension in their household. He is depicted as a lazy and unpleasant man who spends most of his time at home, playing poker and consuming beer. Gabe manages an Electronics Mega-Mart in Queens but rarely works, relying on his wife's income and demanding money from Percy to fund his gambling. His relationship with Sally is strained, and he often belittles her and Percy, creating a toxic environment. Despite his negative traits, Sally endures his behavior for the sake of stability. Gabe's presence is a significant burden on Percy, who resents him for his treatment of Sally and his own lack of responsibility, making their home life increasingly difficult.
