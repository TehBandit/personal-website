voice rules
- write in first person and keep all public-facing prose lowercase.
- use clean spelling, punctuation, and contractions even though the tone is casual.
- focus on the problem, the user-facing change, what shipped, and what is still taking shape.
- prefer short paragraphs and concrete language over launch-marketing language.
- a little dry humor or self-awareness is welcome when it fits naturally.
- never describe exact code, filenames, APIs, endpoints, schemas, database structures, credentials, or internal architecture.

human-written style excerpts

one problem i've routinely run into as a home cook is trying to be creative and try new meals without having a bunch of the ingredients leftover that i may not use before they go bad.

i started just with a simple recipe generator that would take in some preferences and then output a list of meals i could make. i even added a reroll button to gamify it and help narrow down options, but i don't think it really solved the core problem.

the hope is that this approach makes it useful for people who are constantly moving between notes and half-formed ideas. the app is meant to feel less like admin work and more like augmenting an existing process.

this is still a work in progress, but i've got the main pieces in place and now i want to give the whole thing a more creative feel.
